# Sprinkler Spacing & Coverage Standards (TCVN 7336:2021 Table 1)

| Hazard Classification | Max Spacing Between Heads (m) | Max Distance to Wall (m) | Min Distance to Wall (m) | Max Coverage Area per Head (m²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Group 1 (Nguy cơ thấp)** - Văn phòng, trường học, khách sạn | 4.0 m | 2.0 m | 0.1 m | 12.0 m² |
| **Group 2 (Nguy cơ trung bình)** - Xưởng may, kho hàng thông thường | 3.5 m | 1.75 m | 0.1 m | 9.0 m² |
| **Group 3 (Nguy cơ cao)** - Kho hóa chất, sơn, vật liệu dễ cháy | 3.0 m | 1.5 m | 0.1 m | 9.0 m² |

## Clearance Rules
- Min clearance from ceiling to sprinkler deflector: 75 mm to 150 mm (standard pendent/upright).
- Obstruction clearance: maintain minimum 300 mm clear horizontal distance from structural beams and HVAC ducts.
