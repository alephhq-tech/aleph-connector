# Sprinkler Pipe Sizing Rules (TCVN 7336:2021 Table 4)

| Number of Sprinkler Heads Fed | Nominal Diameter (DN) | Internal Diameter Approx (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 head | DN 25 | 27.2 mm |
| 2 heads | DN 32 | 35.9 mm |
| 3 heads | DN 40 | 41.8 mm |
| 4 – 5 heads | DN 50 | 53.0 mm |
| 6 – 8 heads | DN 65 | 68.8 mm |
| 9 – 15 heads | DN 80 | 80.8 mm |
| > 15 heads / Mains | DN 100+ | 105.3 mm |

## Design Rules
- Friction factor: Hazen-Williams C = 120 (steel pipe).
- Maximum water velocity in mains: <= 10 m/s.
- Maximum water velocity through valves/meters: <= 5 m/s.
