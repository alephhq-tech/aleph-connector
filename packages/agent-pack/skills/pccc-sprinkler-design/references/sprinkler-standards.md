# Sprinkler Spacing & Coverage Standards (TCVN 7336:2021 Table 1)

| Hazard Classification | Max Spacing Between Heads (m) | Max Distance to Wall — unprotected (m) | Min Distance to Wall (m) | Max Coverage Area per Head (m²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Group 1 (Nguy cơ thấp)** — văn phòng, khách sạn, trường học | 4.0 m | 2.0 m (K0/K1, nửa spacing) / 1.2 m (K2, K3, khác) | 0.1 m | 12.0 m² |
| **Group 2 (Nguy cơ trung bình)** — xưởng may, kho hàng, gara | 4.0 m | 2.0 m (K0/K1) / 1.2 m (K2, K3, khác) | 0.1 m | 9.0 m² |
| **Group 3 (Nguy cơ cao)** — kho hóa chất, sơn, vật liệu dễ cháy | 4.0 m | 2.0 m (K0/K1) / 1.2 m (K2, K3, khác) | 0.1 m | 9.0 m² |

All Groups 1–3 share the 4.0 m max head-to-head spacing (TCVN 7336:2021 Table 1). The
differences are density (I = 0.08 / 0.12 / 0.24 l/(s·m²)), calculated area (S = 60 / 120 / 120 m²),
coverage per head (12.0 / 9.0 / 9.0 m²) and discharge time (30 / 60 / 60 min).

## Clearance Rules
- Min head-to-head spacing: **1.5 m** (a head must not be soaked/cooled by its neighbour).
- Ceiling-to-deflector clearance: **0.08 m – 0.30 m** (standard pendent/upright).
- Min distance from head to an unprotected wall: 0.1 m; max distance: **2.0 m** for K0/K1 wall classes (half spacing) and **1.2 m** for K2/K3 and other classes (5.2.22).
- Max height of protected space (standard sprinkler): **20 m**.
- Obstruction clearance: maintain minimum 300 mm clear horizontal distance from structural beams and HVAC ducts.
- Obstructions with vertical depth > 0.3 m split the ceiling into independent cells; each cell is protected on its own.
